# BizzyCar

> BizzyCar is a B2B SaaS platform for automotive dealerships that automates service recall management, customer outreach and mobile service. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Saint Peters, Missouri, it ingests VIN-level open-recall data from OEM partners, matches it against a dealer's market area and DMS records, then uses AI-driven outreach to book service appointments and dispatch mobile service vans.

BizzyCar publishes **no public API, developer portal, API reference, SDK or machine-readable
contract**. It is an API *consumer*: the product's value is its pre-built connectors into third-party
dealer management systems and service schedulers. `api.bizzycar.com` exists and serves the company's
own applications, but it is undocumented and unauthenticated requests receive only a JSON error
envelope. Do not represent BizzyCar as having a developer program.

## Products

- [Recall Outreach](https://www.bizzycar.com/recall-outreach): Automated identification of open recalls in a dealer's market area, customer outreach, and appointment booking.
- [Recall Scout](https://www.bizzycar.com/recall-scout): Recall discovery against inbound and existing service traffic.
- [Recall Radar](https://www.bizzycar.com/recall-radar): Recall monitoring surface.
- [Service Engine](https://www.bizzycar.com/service-engine): Converts service milestones, declined work and inspections mined from DMS data into booked appointments.
- [Mobile Service](https://www.bizzycar.com/mobile-service): Scheduling, routing and management for dealer mobile service vans performing repairs at the customer's location.
- [Fleet IQ](https://www.bizzycar.com/fleet-iq): Fleet-focused recall and service management.

## Integrations

BizzyCar consumes other providers' APIs rather than exposing its own. Published connector targets:

- Dealer management systems: Dealertrack, DMS Plus, Open/Mate, Asbury, PBS, Tekion, Fortellis, Reynolds & Reynolds
- Service schedulers: Xtime, TCC, DealerFX, Affinitiv, Update Promise
- Recall data: OEM partner feeds, VIN-level, refreshed daily
