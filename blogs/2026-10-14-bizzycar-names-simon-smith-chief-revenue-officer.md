BizzyCar, the AI-powered automotive SaaS company and the #1 leader in recall solutions, today announced that Simon Smith has joined the company as Chief Revenue Officer. Simon will lead BizzyCar’s Go-to-Market organization. BizzyCar built its position by helping dealerships and OEMs close open recalls through two-way text messaging powered by its patented AI agent.
