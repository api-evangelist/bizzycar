Recall Date: October 8, 2026 NHTSA ID: 26V664 Mfr. Campaign Number: 308/035G Manufacturer: Hyundai Motor America Affected Components: Switch Assy Power Seat, Driver (LH), Part Number 88990-B1030, and Switch Assy Power Seat, Assistant (RH), Part Number 88991-B1000. Both are front seat slider and height adjustment control switches on non-memory power seats, supplied by Nidec Mobility Korea Corporation.
